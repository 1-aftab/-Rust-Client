# Rust Client Privacy Policy

**Project:** Rust Client  
**Provided by:** the Rust Client maintainers (**we**, **us**)  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Last updated:** November 11, 2026

## 1. Summary

Rust Client works on your device. We do not run a user-account system, we do not include advertising or analytics software, and the app does not send your information to a server of ours. We do not sell personal information.

Rust Client does use the network. When you sign in, search, download, play online or open a web page, your device talks to third parties. They receive what such a connection always reveals, such as your IP address, and whatever the feature needs to send.

## 2. Information kept on your device

- **Profiles and accounts:** profile names, identifiers, skins, and the tokens an authentication server issued to you.
- **Game data:** game files, worlds, resource packs, shader packs, mods, screenshots and options.
- **Launcher data:** settings, control layouts, download caches, logs and crash reports.

This information stays in the app's storage on your device. Other apps cannot read the app's private storage unless your device is rooted or you share the files yourself. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.

## 3. Third parties your device contacts

Depending on what you do, your device connects to:

- **Game file servers** (Mojang / Microsoft) to download the files a game needs.
- **Authentication servers** you choose, to sign in. Your credentials go to that server, not to us.
- **Content platforms** (for example Modrinth or CurseForge) and mirrors, to search and download mods, packs and similar content.
- **Runtime and component hosts**, to download Java runtimes and other components.
- **Game servers and multiplayer services** you join. They receive your profile name, identifier and IP address, and what you do in the game.
- **Log-upload services**, only when you choose to upload a log. Logs can contain device details, file paths and profile names; check a log before sharing it.
- **Web pages** you open from the app, in your browser.

Each of these parties handles information under its own privacy policy, which we do not control. If you enter a server address that uses unencrypted HTTP, what you send to it can be read on the network.

## 4. Permissions

Rust Client asks only for what its features need, such as network access, notifications for long downloads, the microphone or storage access when a feature you turn on requires it. You can refuse or revoke a permission in your device's settings; the feature that needs it may then not work.

## 5. Children

Rust Client is not directed at children and has no account system of ours through which a child's information could be collected. Online play and third-party services can involve personal information; a parent or guardian should supervise a minor's use.

## 6. Your choices

- Delete profiles, logs and caches in the app, or all data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling.
- For information held by an account provider, a content platform, a game server or another third party, contact that party.

## 7. Changes

When this Policy changes, the app shows the new version and asks for your consent again.
